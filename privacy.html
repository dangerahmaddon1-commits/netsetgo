Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains how Net Set Go (“we,” “our,” or “the site”) handles information when you use our browser-based internet speed test. The tool does not require an account and does not currently offer a saved test-history feature.

Information processed when you use the tool

  • Speed test: when you start a test, your browser sends and receives test data through Cloudflare’s speed-test endpoints to estimate latency, download speed, and upload speed. The service receiving those requests may process technical information such as your IP address, request details, and timestamps under its own policies.
  • Network details: when the page loads, it requests information from ipapi.co to show your public IP address and network/provider information in the advanced details panel. That third-party service may receive your IP address and request metadata.
  • Basic site analytics: Vercel Analytics is included to help us understand aggregate site usage and performance. Its processing is governed by Vercel’s applicable privacy documentation.
  • Browser information: your browser and device necessarily provide technical information to the site and to services used to deliver its features.

Advertising and cookies

Net Set Go includes Google AdSense publisher code while the site is being reviewed. The presence of this code does not mean the site or account has been approved, and ads are not intentionally placed by this page. If Google serves ads on this site in the future, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to deliver, measure, limit, and personalize advertising as permitted by applicable settings and law. Third parties may place or read cookies in your browser or use web beacons or IP addresses as part of ad serving.

For information about how Google uses data on partner sites, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can manage Google ad personalization through Google My Ad Center. Your browser also provides controls for cookies and site data.

How we use information

Information processed by the site and its service providers is used to run the speed test, display network diagnostics you request, monitor site reliability, understand general usage, protect the service, and—if advertising is enabled in the future—support advertising and measurement.

Retention and sharing

Test results are displayed in your browser; the site does not currently provide an account or a saved test-history feature. We do not control the retention practices of Cloudflare, ipapi.co, Vercel, Google, or other third-party services. Review their policies for details about their processing and retention.

Children

Net Set Go is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the site or its services change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
