How Net Set Go Measures Internet Speed

Net Set Go tests your broadband, Wi-Fi, or mobile network performance by simulating real-world data streams. It measures your latency (ping), download bandwidth, and upload capacity to determine your exact internet service provider (ISP) speed.

What Is My IP Address & ISP?

Our advanced diagnostics automatically detect your public IP address and network provider location to route test packets through the nearest high-speed regional server node.
