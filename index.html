How Net Set Go Measures Internet Speed

Net Set Go is a browser-based diagnostic tool for checking the performance of the connection your device is currently using. The test sends and receives data through Cloudflare's global edge network and calculates transfer rates from the amount of data moved and the elapsed time. Results are estimates of performance during this test, not a guarantee of the speed your internet provider advertises.

Download speed

Download speed estimates how quickly data reaches your device. It matters for loading websites, streaming video, downloading files, and receiving updates. The test transfers a sample payload and calculates megabits per second (Mbps). Very fast connections, browser limitations, or short tests can affect the result.

Upload speed

Upload speed estimates how quickly your device sends data to the test endpoint. It can affect video calls, sending large files, cloud backups, and live streaming. The measurement depends on your connection and the route between your device and the endpoint.

Ping and jitter

Ping is the time a request takes to reach the test service and return, measured in milliseconds (ms). Lower latency is generally better for responsive gaming and calls. Jitter describes variation in latency between samples. Net Set Go shows a simple estimate from a small sample; it does not measure packet loss.

How to Get a More Reliable Result

Pause large downloads, streaming, cloud backups, and other speed tests before starting. For the most consistent comparison, use the same device and network, close unnecessary browser tabs, and repeat the test a few times. If possible, compare Wi-Fi with a wired Ethernet connection. Wi-Fi distance, walls, interference, router capability, device performance, VPNs, and network congestion can all change results.

How to Understand Your Results

Mbps means megabits per second; it is not the same as megabytes per second (MB/s). Divide Mbps by eight for a rough MB/s conversion before accounting for overhead. A higher download number is not the only sign of a good connection: latency, stability, upload capacity, and the requirements of your activity matter too.

What Is My IP Address and ISP?

The optional advanced details panel requests network information from ipapi.co to display the public IP address and network/provider information returned by that service. This information may be incomplete or approximate. Your public IP can identify a network connection; it is not the same as a device's private local address.

Limitations and Privacy

Results are affected by your device, browser, local network, ISP routing, the test endpoint, and current congestion. Net Set Go does not promise a particular speed or diagnose your provider's network. The speed test sends sample data to Cloudflare's test endpoint, and the advanced details panel contacts ipapi.co. Read our Privacy Policy for information about third-party services and advertising technologies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my result lower than my internet plan?

Your plan may describe a maximum or typical access speed, while this test measures performance at one moment from your device to an external endpoint. Wi-Fi conditions, congestion, background traffic, and device limits can reduce the measured result.

Should I use Wi-Fi or Ethernet?

Ethernet is usually more consistent for testing because it avoids wireless interference. Wi-Fi testing is still useful when you want to understand the performance you actually experience on your phone or laptop.

Does Net Set Go store my test results?

The current tool displays results in your browser and does not provide an account or a saved test-history feature. Third-party services contacted by the page may process technical request data under their own policies. See the Privacy Policy for details.
